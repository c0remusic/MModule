# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude Code in this repository.

## Project

MModule is the repository of MoDule (called MD Drums up to 0.1.1; the code keeps that name: `mdDrums`, `mdDrumsPlugin`): the Elektron Machinedrum's synthesis engines as a plug-in built like The Usual Suspects' Gearmulator plug-ins (**TUS**: Osirus, OsTIrus, Vavra, Xenia, Nodal Red 2x, JE-8086) and edited like **Overbridge**. One window edits the machine tab by tab: each track's machine, synthesis, effects, routing and LFO, the master effects, the mix, the Kit. Every parameter is a host parameter that Live can automate, and Live's clips sequence the 16 tracks. OS 1.63's code runs piecewise: its voice and master programs on emulated DSP56300s (`source/dsp56300/`), its machine, LFO and smoothing routines in an emulated 68k (`source/mc68k/`); the OS itself, its boot and its sequencer do not run.

The TUS architecture, with OsTIrus as the reference (`osTIrusJucePlugin/` over `virusJucePlugin/` in upstream Gearmulator, not in this repository): a thin plug-in over the emulated machine, its parameters and their MIDI messages declared in the JSON (`midipackets`, `regions`), presets in the Patch Manager, a skin whose controls bind to parameters. MoDule keeps its parameters in its JSON but maps them to the Machinedrum's messages in C++ (`mdProtocol/mdautomation.*`, `mdDrums/mdDrumsMessages.*`), and keeps its Kits in its own Bank rather than the Patch Manager.

**Latency** is a requirement, measured rather than assumed: the time from a host note to the sound at the plug-in's output, and its jitter. A change to the trig path or the render pipeline reports both, before and after.

Gearmulator MD (md-mm) drives the intact firmware over MIDI: notes trigger tracks, host parameters reach it as CC, Kits travel as SysEx. Engines without the UC were ruled out in `.scratch/parallel-transport/issues/07-go-no-go.md` on CPU and effort: the sequencer costs about 6 points of UC, dropping the UC leaves DSP1 (the busiest core) as heavy, and the DSPs' HI08 protocol would need reverse engineering machine by machine. **Decided otherwise on 2026-10-06: the plug-in moves to the engines without the OS.** The protocol is now documented in public (`.scratch/uc-less-engines/`), and Machinemodule's engine (sd88me, AGPL-3.0), imported as `source/elektron/md/mdEngine/`, runs OS 1.63's engines without it: DSP2 emulated, the OS's own machine, LFO and smoothing routines called in a 68k emulator, DSP1's track chain and mix in C++. Measured against the full firmware: the same samples (`mdEngineFirmwareTest`), about a tenth of the CPU, 0.48 ms of note-to-sound latency against ~5.4 ms (Machinemodule's measure, from the 32-sample block that holds the note; from the note itself, 1.16 ms on average and 0.68 ms of jitter on TRX-BD, whose voice sounds a block after its trigger), and no 14 s silent boot. Since ticket 19 MoDule's Hits are sample-accurate: every Track's share of the mix waits the rest of the engine's block, so a Hit sounds 31 samples after its note plus its voice's own start, and since ticket 36 every voice waits for the latest, TRX-BD's, whose voice sounds a block after its trigger: each machine's start (`Engine::voiceStart`, measured by `mdDrumsEngineTest` on every machine's own output: 4 to 8 samples for most, 12 for ROM, 36 for TRX-BD and TRX-S2) is held back to `Engine::AlignedStart`, so that every Hit sounds `Engine::HitDelay` samples after its note on Main and on its Out alike (83, 1.88 ms, no jitter at 44.1 kHz; the Outs wait Dynamix's 16 samples as Main does). TRX-XT, TRX-XC and TRX-CB start later still (40, 68, 102) and are not held back, as on the Machinedrum. A Choke cuts its target exactly where the cutting Hit sounds, a lower Track at the engine's next tick instead, as `HostModel` reports each block's cuts (ticket 38). Since ticket 24 the master effects run the mixer DSP's own master section on a second emulated DSP56300 (`mdEngine/engine/MasterEngine.*`) with the words the OS's own code computes, sample for sample the firmware's (`mdEngineFirmwareTest`); Main comes 16 samples after the Outs, Dynamix's look-ahead, as on the machine. The combined work is AGPL v3 (`NOTICE.md`). The full firmware still runs here, as the oracle: `mdLib` and the tests in `mdLibTest/`.

The repository split on 2026-10-06 from [c0remusic/gearmulator-md-mm](https://github.com/c0remusic/gearmulator-md-mm), which keeps the full-machine plug-ins (sequencer editor, BIBLIO's patterns, pattern chains), and became MModule on 2026-10-17: [c0remusic/gearmulator-md-drums](https://github.com/c0remusic/gearmulator-md-drums)' history filtered with `git filter-repo` to MoDule, the firmware oracle and what they build on. The other Gearmulator synths, md-mm's full-machine plug-in (`mdJucePlugin`) and its tests are not in this repository; gearmulator-md-drums keeps release 0.1.0. The Monomachine is out of this project: the Monomachine paths left in `mdProtocol` and `mdLib` serve `mdLib` alone.

## Where things are

- `source/elektron/md/mdDrums/`: without JUCE. `mdDrums::Engine`, mdEngine as the plug-in drives it (flash lookup, 32-sample block rendering, Links, Chokes, LFOs); `mdDrums::Device`, the `synthLib::Device` over it; `mdDrumsMessages.*`, MoDule's own messages, its state format and the host parameters as the Machinedrum's messages. `mdDrumsPlugin/`: the processor, the Controller, the editor (its custom elements and views), the parameter JSON, the skins, and `Design/` (the approved v22 mockup and its boxes).
- `source/elektron/md/mdEngine/`: the engines without the OS (`EngineT` in `engine/MdEngine.h`), with its origin and local changes in `README.md`. Musashi's entry points serve one CPU class per executable, so nothing links it with `mdLib`'s `Microcontroller`: tests that compare the two run in separate processes.
- `source/elektron/md/mdProtocol/`: the machines' MIDI without an emulator, linked by `mdLib` and MoDule. `mdautomation.*` maps host parameters to CC; `mdmachines.*` is the machine table; `mdsysexautomation.*` holds the SysEx codecs (Kits, patterns, `$5B`, master effects, `$62`); `mdkit.*` is the whole Kit (`MdKit`), its dump writer and reader and the Link and Choke messages (`$65`, `$66`); `mdkitchange.*` decodes, encodes and applies, without allocating, the messages that change one thing of the Kit (`$5B`, `$5D-$60`, `$62`, `$65`, `$66`) as OS 1.63 reads them, which the Device and `messages::parameterMessage` use and the allocating encoders wrap (ticket 39).
- `source/elektron/md/mdLib/`: the whole machine, MoDule's oracle (from md-mm). `mdhardware.*` runs the UC and the DSPs and delivers MIDI; `mddevice.*` is the `synthLib::Device`. Nothing of MoDule links it.
- Tests: next to their code (`mdDrums/*Test.cpp`, `mdDrumsPlugin/*Test.cpp`, `mdProtocol/mdProtocolKitTest.cpp`), and the firmware comparisons in `mdLibTest/` (`mdEngineFirmwareTest` and its fixtures, `mdKitDumpFirmwareTest`, `mdAutomationMidiTest`).
- Shared Gearmulator layers: `synthLib/` (Device, MIDI), `jucePluginLib/` (parameters, MIDI Learn, Patch Manager), `jucePluginEditorLib/`, `juceRmlUi/` (RmlUi inside JUCE).

## Build

Windows, Visual Studio 2022, build directory `temp/cmake_vs22`, VST3 only:

```powershell
cmake . -B temp/cmake_vs22 -G "Visual Studio 17 2022" -DBUILD_TESTING=ON `
  -Dgearmulator_BUILD_JUCEPLUGIN_AU=OFF -Dgearmulator_BUILD_JUCEPLUGIN_CLAP=OFF -Dgearmulator_BUILD_JUCEPLUGIN_LV2=OFF `
  -Dgearmulator_BUILD_JUCEPLUGIN_Standalone=OFF -Dgearmulator_BUILD_JUCEPLUGIN_VST2=OFF -Dgearmulator_BUILD_FX_PLUGIN=OFF
cmake --build temp/cmake_vs22 --config Release -j 6 --target mdDrumsPlugin_VST3
```

A release build adds `"-DMD_DRUMS_VERSION=X.Y.Z"`, quoted: unquoted, PowerShell cuts the argument at its first dot and the build says version 0, which `mdDrumsUpdateTest` refuses. The macOS build (universal VST3 and AU) is `.github/workflows/md-drums-macos.yml`, run by hand.

- `jucePluginLib/pluginVersion.cpp` recompiles on every build (version timestamp, MSB8065 by design): a build with no change still compiles one file.
- Run firmware executables by hand only under a wall-clock guard (`Start-Process -PassThru` + `WaitForExit(ms)`, kill on timeout).

## Tests

- A test that creates a processor makes it for tests (`mdDrums::Processor(true)`): no update check, no MCP server, whose firewall prompt Windows would show for every new executable.
- A harness that paces a fake audio callback spin-waits to each deadline: a process without a visible window runs on the 15.6 ms timer, and a sleeping audio thread invents dropouts.
- A new test's `main` sets `setvbuf(stdout, nullptr, _IONBF, 0)`: `_IOLBF` crashes the Microsoft runtime at startup (0xc0000409).
- An editor test is the editor's clock (`Editor::setClock`): it steps `Editor::frame` as a display tick, and an RmlUi update runs the frame first, as in the plug-in.
- MoDule's two host tests share `mdDrumsPlugin/mdDrumsHostDriver.h` (header-only, JUCE alone: the VST3 test does not link the plug-in): notes played in blocks, a parameter automated, a state restored, and the scenarios of ticket 34 that `mdDrumsProcessorTest` (in process) and `mdDrumsVst3HostTest` (through VST3 hosting) both run, each adapter counting what the plug-in tells the host as its channel reports it.

## Critical implementation details

- **State:** `Device::getState()` appends to `_state` with `insert()`, never `assign()`: the Plugin layer has already written its version header there.
- **Host:** what the plug-in changes by itself reaches the host one of two ways (ticket 34, measured in Live 12.2.1). An edit: JUCE turns a parameter's listeners called on the message thread into `performEdit`, which Live records while it records and adds to the device's panel. A re-read: `PresetChange`, then `Controller::tellHost` (`updateHostDisplay` with `programChanged`, VST3's `restartComponent(kParamValuesChanged)`; this JUCE's `getParamNormalized` reads the parameter), after which Live, while it records, writes automation for every parameter on its panel, changed or not (Live sets no `kRecording` to wait for). Values changed in bulk (a Kit, a state, a machine's 24 parameters) are re-read; one parameter following another (a relay and its parameter of the Track shown) is not told, since as an edit Live takes the relay for the parameter last touched in the editor; the relays are edits without a gesture when the Track shown changes; nothing at load.
- **Device lock:** `Plugin::withDeviceLocked` pauses rendering, which the host shows as a CPU spike when it runs on every frame or click: the editor reads the Telemetry and the parameters instead.
- **Threads:** RmlUi DOM changes happen on the JUCE message thread. From audio or MIDI callbacks, post with `juce::MessageManager::callAsync`, the lambda guarded by the static instance-set pattern against use-after-free.
- **Editor frame:** MoDule's views change the document only in an RmlUi event (a click, a key, a drag: they write at once) or in `Editor::frame`, both under the document's access lock, which the OpenGL thread renders under. In the frame each view's `step` reads what it shows (parameters, Telemetry, Controller, Bank), compares it with what it drew, writes only what differs and says whether it wrote; no view keeps a timer or a parameter listener. The frame runs before every RmlUi update (`RmlComponent::evPreUpdate`), so what an event changed shows in the frame that draws the event; at the display's rate while the window shows (one `juce::TimedCallback`, for the host's automation, the Telemetry, a Track shown by Push or a state); at once when a click shows another Track (`Editor::setCurrentPart`) or a file has been chosen. A frame with nothing new writes nothing, and costs about a microsecond (`mdDrumsSkinTest`'s "frame").
- **Animation:** the software renderer ignores RmlUi transforms (`RendererJuce::SetTransform` stores the matrix, nothing reads it). Draw what moves with `juceRmlUi::ElemCanvas` (see `mdDrumsMeterView.cpp`, `mdDrumsHitView.cpp`), and hide elements with `display`, never a zero scale.
- **Environment overrides** in `mdLib` (`MD_*`, `MDMM_*`) go through `mdLib/mdenv.h` (`envOverride`, `envNumber`, `envCount<T>`), where unset, empty and unparsable all count as unset: PowerShell's `SetEnvironmentVariable($n, $null)` leaves an empty variable that child processes see.

## Code conventions

- Tabs (size 4), 120 columns, braces on their own line, namespace content indented; `.clang-format` in `source/`.
- PascalCase classes; camelCase functions, variables and namespaces; `m_` members, `_` parameters (`void func(int _param)`).
- Early returns over nesting. C++17.
- Commit subjects are imperative sentences that say what the change does for the machine ("Write a step edit into the pattern edited, not into A01"); the body says why, with the numbers measured.
