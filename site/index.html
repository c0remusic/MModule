Machinedrum synthesis on emulated DSPs, as a plug-in

MD Drums runs the code of the Elektron Machinedrum's OS 1.63 machines in any VST3 or AU host, with the voice DSP playing the 16 tracks and the mixer DSP the master effects. Your DAW sends the notes and can automate any of the 608 parameters, while one window edits the whole Kit the way Overbridge does.

Download for Windows or macOS Read the source

Install

Windows

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Copy the MD Drums.vst3 folder to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\.
  3. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW and load MD Drums (c0remusic) on an instrument track.

macOS

  1. Open the .pkg. macOS refuses it the first time because Apple hasn't notarized it.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about the package and click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password. On macOS 15 Sequoia this is the only way: right-click, Open no longer gets past it.
  3. Run the installer; it puts the VST3 and the AU in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/.
  4. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW and load MD Drums (c0remusic).

With the zip instead, copy the bundles to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ and ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/, then clear the quarantine flag in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"MD Drums.vst3"
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"MD Drums.component"
Then give MD Drums your Machinedrum UW flash image with OS 1.63, the 8 MB file elektron_sps1-1uw_os1.63.bin that Gearmulator MD reads too. Elektron owns it, so it doesn't ship with MD Drums, which is meant for people who own the hardware; please don't ask for the file.

The simplest way is from the plug-in: load MD Drums, click No Machinedrum flash image: choose the file in its top bar (or right-click the editor and choose Load Machinedrum flash image...) and pick the file. MD Drums checks it, plays at once and copies it into its own folder, so it finds it on its own next time.

Or put the file, with exactly that name, in Documents/c0remusic/MD Drums/roms/ (create the folders) before loading the plug-in: C:\Users\you\Documents\c0remusic\MD Drums\roms\ on Windows, ~/Documents/c0remusic/MD Drums/roms/ on macOS.

What you get

  • 16 trackswith every machine OS 1.63 has; the ROM machines play the UW sample bank in your flash image.
  • The master effectsRhythm Echo, Gate Box, EQ and Dynamix, running on the mixer DSP's own code.
  • An editorwith Track, Mix and Master tabs and a machine browser you can hear before you choose.
  • Kitsin a bank of 64 slots that starts with the 16 factory Kits and reads and writes .syx dumps.
  • 16 outputsMain, then Out 01 to Out 15; any track can go to any of them.

How it plays

  • Notes 36 to 51play tracks 1 to 16 with their velocity, and every Hit lands 1.88 ms after its note, whatever the machine.
  • Lockscome from notes 64 to 87: each sets one parameter for a single Hit, its velocity giving the value.
  • Push 2gets a page of eight per track, or relays that follow whichever track the editor shows.
