Machinedrum synthesis on emulated DSPs, as a plug-in

MoDule runs the code of the Elektron Machinedrum's OS 1.63 machines in any VST3 or AU host, with the voice DSP playing the 16 tracks and the mixer DSP the master effects. Your DAW sends the notes and can automate any of the 608 parameters, while one window edits the whole Kit the way Overbridge does.

Download for Windows or macOS Read the source

You'll need your own Machinedrum UW firmware, the 8 MB flash image with OS 1.63; it doesn't come with MoDule. Why, and how to load it

Up to 0.1.1 it was called MD Drums: the same plug-in, and your sets, Kits and firmware carry over.

md-drums-seq

Max for Live, Windows

A step sequencer for Live 12, written for MoDule: Locks, conditions, ratchets, micro-timing and swing over 16 Patterns, with each Track named after the machine your Kit puts on it. It can also print a Pattern into clips. Windows only for now; it needs Max for Live.

Install

Windows

  1. Run the installer. It isn't signed, so Windows SmartScreen may say it protected your PC: click More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Let it install; it puts MoDule.vst3 in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\, where every VST3 host looks.
  3. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW and load MoDule (c0remusic) on an instrument track.

With the zip instead, copy its MoDule.vst3 folder to that same place yourself. Up to 0.1.1 the files are called MD Drums.

macOS

  1. Open the .pkg. macOS refuses it the first time because Apple hasn't notarized it.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about the package and click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password. On macOS 15 Sequoia this is the only way: right-click, Open no longer gets past it.
  3. Run the installer; it puts the VST3 and the AU in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/.
  4. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW and load MoDule (c0remusic).

With the zip instead, copy the bundles to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ and ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/, then clear the quarantine flag in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/MoDule.vst3
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/MoDule.component

md-drums-seq: unzip it and put its md-drums-seq folder in Live's User Library, under Presets/MIDI Effects/Max MIDI Effect/, then restart Live. Put MD Drums Seq on a MIDI track in front of MoDule.

Then give MoDule your Machinedrum UW firmware: the 8 MB flash image with OS 1.63, the same one Gearmulator MD reads (not an OS update in .syx). Elektron owns it, so it doesn't ship with MoDule, which is meant for people who own the hardware; please don't ask for the file.

Load MoDule, click No Machinedrum flash image: choose the file in its top bar (or right-click the editor and choose Load Machinedrum flash image...) and pick your file, whatever its name. MoDule checks it, plays at once and keeps a copy in its own folder, so it finds it on its own next time.

What you get

  • 16 trackswith every machine OS 1.63 has; the ROM machines play the UW sample bank in your flash image.
  • The master effectsRhythm Echo, Gate Box, EQ and Dynamix, running on the mixer DSP's own code.
  • An editorwith Track, Mix and Master tabs and a machine browser you can hear before you choose.
  • Kitsin a bank of 64 slots that starts with the 16 factory Kits and reads and writes .syx dumps.
  • 16 outputsMain, then Out 01 to Out 15; any track can go to any of them.

How it plays

  • Notes 36 to 51play tracks 1 to 16 with their velocity, and every Hit lands 1.88 ms after its note, whatever the machine.
  • Lockscome from notes 64 to 87: each sets one parameter for a single Hit, its velocity giving the value.
  • Push 2gets a page of eight per track, or relays that follow whichever track the editor shows.
